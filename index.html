About Digital Art

Digital art is one of the most versatile creative fields of our time. At its core, digital art refers to any artistic work that uses digital technology as an essential part of the creative or presentation process. Examples range from illustrations painted on a drawing tablet to 3D sculptures rendered in software. Altogether, digital art encompasses an enormous range of styles, techniques, and intentions.

What makes digital art so compelling is its accessibility and flexibility. Unlike traditional media, digital tools allow artists to undo mistakes instantly, experiment with unlimited color palettes and tools, and share their work with a global audience in seconds. Software like Adobe Photoshop, Procreate, Clip Studio Paint, and Blender has given access to art creation globally.

The history of digital art stretches back to the 1960s when early computer scientists began experimenting with machine generated images. Artists like Harold Cohen and Vera Molnar pioneered algorithmic and computer-assisted art. Today, digital art has exploded into mainstream culture, from concept art for blockbuster films and video games, to NFT collections selling for millions of dollars, even to the illustrations and animations we scroll past every day on social media.

This website is a celebration of that creative world. Whether you're a seasoned illustrator looking to go digital, or a complete beginner curious about picking up a stylus for the first time, there's something here for you. Explore the gallery to preview different styles and techniques, learn about the tools professionals use, and find inspiration for your own creative practice. Digital art isn't just a medium, it's a community, a career path, and for many people, a lifeline of creative expression.
